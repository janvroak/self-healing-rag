# Veridian Dynamics Employee Handbook (2026 edition)

Veridian Dynamics is a robotics company headquartered in Pune, India, with a second office in Tallinn, Estonia. It was founded in 2019 by Meera Kulkarni and Jonas Tamm.

## Working hours and remote work

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time. Outside core hours, employees choose their own schedule.
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a department director and are reviewed every six months.
Employees working remotely from outside their home country for more than 30 days in a calendar year must notify the People Operations team, because of tax and visa rules.

## Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at 2 days per month.
Up to 8 unused leave days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March if not used.
Sick leave is separate from annual leave: employees get 12 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive working days.
Parental leave is 26 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks at full pay for the secondary caregiver.

## Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 60,000 INR (or 700 EUR for the Tallinn office) for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Requests above 25,000 INR need manager approval.

## Equipment

New employees choose between a 14-inch or 16-inch laptop. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Employees may buy their old laptop for 10% of its original price when it is refreshed.
