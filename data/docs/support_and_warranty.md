# Customer Support and Warranty Policy

## Warranty

All Veridian Dynamics hardware comes with a 2-year limited warranty from the date of delivery. Batteries are covered for 12 months or 200 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
The warranty does not cover crash damage, water damage beyond the device's IP rating, or damage caused by unofficial firmware.

## Veridian Care+

Veridian Care+ is an optional protection plan that costs 690 USD per year per drone. It includes up to two crash replacements per year, with a 250 USD service fee per replacement, and priority support.

## Support tiers and response times

- Standard support (all customers): email only, first response within 2 business days.
- Priority support (Care+ customers): email and phone, first response within 4 business hours.
- Enterprise support (contracts of 20 or more drones): a named technical account manager and a 1-hour response time for critical incidents, 24/7.

## Returns

Unused hardware can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened hardware that has flown can be returned within 14 days with a 15% restocking fee.

## Repairs

Out-of-warranty repairs are handled at the Pune service centre. Standard repair turnaround is 10 business days. Customers in the EU send devices to the Tallinn office, where turnaround is 7 business days.
