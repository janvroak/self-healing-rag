# Company FAQ

## How big is Veridian Dynamics?

As of September 2026, Veridian Dynamics has 312 employees: 241 in Pune and 71 in Tallinn.

## Who are the main customers?

Most customers are energy utilities, wind farm operators and bridge inspection agencies. The largest customer is the Baltic wind farm operator NordVind, which runs 140 Kestrel drones.

## Has the company raised funding?

Veridian Dynamics raised a 4 million USD seed round in 2020 and a 22 million USD Series A in 2023, led by Lakeshore Ventures.

## What is the Kestrel K3?

The Kestrel K3 is announced for 2027. The company has said it will support swarm inspections with up to five drones flying one coordinated mission. Pricing has not been announced.

## Does the company have a bug bounty?

Yes. Security researchers can report vulnerabilities to security@veridian.example. Rewards range from 200 USD to 10,000 USD depending on severity. Testing must not involve drones in flight.

## Office perks

Both offices provide free lunch on weekdays. The Pune office has an indoor flight test cage, and the Tallinn office has a sauna.
